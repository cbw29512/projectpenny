# Achievement Center v0.1
Migration 030 adds append-only disclosure reviews for three provisional
achievement definitions. First Dollar: net recorded commercial receipts
at least 100 USD cents, not community support or founder funding.
In the Green: positive net commercial receipts and positive commercial
result after recorded costs and fees; not audited profit.
Proof of Concept: a reviewed unsuppressed terminal experiment that met
its target, not proof of sales or product launch.
Finance requires a fresh matching reconciliation and current exact
reviewed financial snapshot; source binds snapshot version and
reconciliation identity. Proof of Concept binds a reviewed timeline
event and rechecks original source integrity. Explicit admin evidence
review of exact binding is mandatory. No automatic awards. Latest
review governs; withdrawal preserves history. Changed sources or
expired finance evidence require review again, not permanent live
eligibility. Exact replay does not reactivate an older review.
Candidate is local only, fixed codes/status/UTC review day, one-hour
maximum freshness bounded by source expiry. Review date is NOT
the first historical date threshold was reached. No private identifiers,
names, actual records, inferred profit, mission percentage, or public
publication. No new model permissions. Synthetic database tests only.
Other milestones (live conversation, launch, recovery, compute upgrade,
million-dollar mission) remain unverified until appropriate evidence
contracts and owner-approved definitions exist. Next: static Achievement
Center consumer and approved publication integration.
