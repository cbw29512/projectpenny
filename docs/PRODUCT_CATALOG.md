# Private commercial catalog and direct financial attribution
Migration 027 registers a product candidate from the exact current passed
Workshop artifact review and verified content hash. One candidate per
artifact; immutable evidence and source references. Any newer quality
review suspends the candidate (new version required for release planning).
Withdrawal is terminal; historical financial attribution remains possible
for refunds, costs and earlier receipts. No price offer or storefront.
Guardian allows trusted administrator catalog review and attribution only.
Attach an original existing Penny commercial receipt, refund, fee,
operating expense or founder-paid expense to one product, with independent
reviewed evidence. No duplicate attribution, partial allocation, support
or founder income as sales. Reversals inherit the original link. No ledger
entry created by attribution. Missing entries report unavailable. Direct
result subtracts attributed refunds/fees/costs; it is not audited profit,
cash balance, total product ROI or proof of actual market demand.
Attribution is immutable in this beta; mistaken links require a future
audited correction workflow. Shared cost allocation, source completeness,
settlement verification, actual payment/order matching, product pricing,
owner-approved release, MFA operator UI and public projection remain gates.
All records/tests synthetic; no production deployment, payment or spend.
