# Penny's product brand — mandatory release contract

Maker direction: Penny is the recognizable brand across everything she creates.
People should receive useful products and be able to recognize and follow the
creator's public entrepreneurial journey. Branding is not a substitute for value.

## Identity and placement

- Use the same Maker-approved canonical Penny character artwork and name on
  every Penny-created product, including beta previews, downloadable deliverables,
  storefront listings, apps, games and physical packaging.
- Printable: visible character/name on cover or first page and restrained
  footer on subsequent pages where appropriate. App/game: recognizable Penny
  character/name in product UI, About and distribution presentation. Physical:
  approved artwork/name on product or packaging appropriate to the format.
- For text-only or accessibility variants include textual Penny attribution;
  graphical primary editions still require the approved artwork. Never silently
  omit the character from the primary deliverable.
- Suggested display credit: "Created by Penny" / "Penny's Workshop".
  Canonical slogan: "Financially Underfunded. Sarcastically Overqualified."
  Slogan, signature treatment and link placement are optional style choices.
- Attribute AI-assisted creation and the human-operated seller accurately.
  Credit verified community contributions with consent. No fictional endorsements,
  false claims of copyright ownership, false professional credentials or purchase
  pressure. A link or QR code must use the verified official destination.
- Existing preview SVG is provisional, not approved final commercial artwork.
  Maker must approve final canonical asset and usage rights before release.

## State and provenance (planned, not installed schema)

BrandAsset: version, approved artwork digest, approved name, rights/evidence
reference, active or revoked status, Maker review.
BrandedEdition: immutable product artifact ID and source digest, brand version,
format, rendered content digest, release review reference, state. Render with
trusted deterministic packaging, never model-selected external asset URLs.
Preview and release are distinct approval scopes; final release binds exact
branded deliverable, listing and approved brand version. Historical private
artifacts and quality-review digests remain intact. New branding creates a
derived edition requiring its own review, not a mutation of old records.

## Office Collection

Virtual items may become physical merchandise using stable design/version
IDs and exact reviewed print editions. Preserve canonical Penny artwork,
name and honest seller attribution; separate founder merchandise accounting
from Penny's business proceeds. No sale authority from a scene display or
community vote. See [office collection](OFFICE_COLLECTION.md).

## Release flow

Private prototype -> independent content review / separate safe beta review ->
trusted branding -> review exact rendered edition and listing -> authorized
preview or final commercial release. No brand approval grants spending or
publication authority. Preserve all other Guardian, legal, financial and
privacy gates. Revocation suppresses future distribution; do not claim to
retract copies already downloaded.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Approved canonical Penny asset and identity survive model/hardware upgrades.
- [ ] Every public Penny product preview, listing and primary delivered edition
  includes visible recognizable Penny character and name.
- [ ] Text-only accessible editions include equivalent attribution.
- [ ] Exact branded bytes are reviewed; missing, unapproved, altered or revoked
  brand assets block release. No unapproved asset fetched at render time.
- [ ] Old artifact digests and review history preserved; version and attribution
  accurate. No invented claims or personal data in branding.
- [ ] Print, mobile, app and accessibility layouts remain usable.
- [ ] First checklist receives a branded edition before public beta/commercial use.
