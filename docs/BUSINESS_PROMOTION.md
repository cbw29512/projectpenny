Migration 024 binds one registered, successfully completed internal
candidate to at most one opportunity. A trusted admin supplies exact
result SHA256, separate independently reviewed evidence, reviewer ID,
explicit confirmation and review deadline. Reparse the stored model
output and compare hash and every typed candidate field. Reject missing,
invalid, changed or stale output. New promotion within seven days of
completion (provisional). Reserve draft: source namespace; generic
manual staging cannot use it. Shared scheduler lock and stage_locked
preserve the existing open-opportunity cap, exact replay and atomic
link creation. Conflicting evidence or review parameters fail. Candidate
status exposes the linked opportunity privately. Promoted opportunity
is still only proposed; separate approve and start transitions remain
mandatory. No spend, execution, public posting, fabricated evidence
or real revenue. Human attestation is not independent authentication
or proof of evidence truth. Live model and deployment not tested.
