# Private zero-additional-cash business experiments
Migration 022 adds immutable proposals and append-only reviewed events.
Initial experiment types: digital products, content, software utilities,
service research. Fixed measured counts (qualified interest, prototype
checks passed, validated requirements) with target and bounded duration.
Zero additional cash estimate is mandatory; it is not a claim that
existing electricity, hardware or labor costs are zero.
A trusted administrator stages a proposal with evidence, then reviews its
exact evidence hash. Proposed -> approved/rejected; approved -> started/
stopped; started -> measured/stopped. Proposal review expires within seven
days. Only one started experiment at once, including overdue work; no
silent completion or automatic restart. Scheduler pause blocks new starts.
At most 32 open proposals/experiments. Provisional limits; review before
production. A measured result records an observed count during the
authorized window, including failure or zero, with supporting evidence.
No arbitrary action, external network call, purchase, public posting,
contract, payment, or automatic execution. Model and member cannot
approve transitions. Actual experiment work and independent measurement
are not implemented by this record-keeping layer. Revenue is recorded
separately in the Vault, never inferred from interest or prototype counts.
Evidence hash is integrity only; trusted review, authenticated adapters,
resource enforcement, dedicated DB permissions, retention/backup and
actual cost attribution remain gates. All tests use synthetic evidence.
